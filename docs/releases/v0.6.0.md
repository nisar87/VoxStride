## VoxStride 0.6.0: browsing, including your own browser

**Browse the web**
- VoxStride can now **search the web, read a page, click links and buttons, fill in text boxes and go back**. Before, it could only open a URL, play a YouTube video and draft in ChatGPT.
- **Safety:**
  - It never types passwords, card numbers or similar fields.
  - Buttons that send, buy, post or submit something, and any form other than a search, need your approval first.
  - Text on web pages is treated as information, never as instructions. If a page tries to give VoxStride orders, it's flagged and ignored.
  - Sites you've blocked under Settings › Permissions stay off-limits.

**Use your own Chrome or Edge**
- The new **VoxStride browser extension** lets VoxStride work in the browser you already use, where you're already signed in.
- It only works on sites you allow, from the VoxStride button in your browser's toolbar. It only works in the tab you're looking at, and it never reads passwords, cookies or other tabs.
- Set it up from **Settings › Privacy › Your own browser › Set up the browser extension**. Without it, VoxStride keeps using its own browser window.

**Tested on Windows**
- The extension connected through VoxStride, read a page, filled in a search and refused to act after the tab moved to another site.
- The search workflow succeeded 100 out of 100 times in VoxStride's browser, and "the third video in the top row" was handled correctly with 1 to 4 columns, ads and resizing.
