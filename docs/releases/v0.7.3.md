# VoxStride 0.7.3

Much faster replies, and a simple "yes" now confirms.

## Faster
- **Gemini thinks less for voice requests.** Gemini 3 models think at full depth by default. That took about 14 seconds before your search even started. VoxStride now asks for low thinking on requests and minimal thinking on transcription, so turns and transcription are much quicker.
- **The voice starts sooner.** VoxStride prepares the first sentence of a reply separately and starts speaking it while it prepares the rest. Previously the whole reply had to be ready first (about 7 seconds).

## Easier to confirm
- When VoxStride asks "Ready to execute?", a short answer on its own now confirms: "yes", "proceed", "go ahead", "do it", "haan", "theek hai", "chalo". It counts only as an answer to that question, never in the middle of an instruction.
- A cough or click right after the question no longer cancels it.
