## VoxStride 0.4.0: file actions, setup, permissions and voice improvements

**Work with your files**
- Ask VoxStride to **find, list, copy, rename, move, create folders and move files to the Recycle Bin** in your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos (plus any folders you allow).
- Moves, renames and recycling always show you the exact list to approve first. Nothing is overwritten. "Undo that" puts moved or renamed files back. Nothing is ever deleted permanently.

**Easier start**
- A new **4-step setup**:
  1. Microphone, with tests for speech, "Hey VoxStride" and VoxStride's voice.
  2. AI key, with a connection test.
  3. Permissions.
  4. A safe demo.
- You can run it again from About.

**Your permissions** (Settings › Privacy)
- Choose which folders VoxStride may use, block apps it must never open, block websites it must never visit, and switch off screenshots completely.

**Voice**
- **Your first words aren't cut off** after "Hey VoxStride": the moment of audio during the switch is kept.
- VoxStride **ignores its own voice**, so it can't wake itself up.
- **Hold-to-talk** option: hold Ctrl+Alt+V while you speak and release to run. A quick tap still works as before.
- **Microphone recovery:** if your microphone disconnects or goes silent, VoxStride reopens it automatically.
- Optional **natural mode:** a pause ends your turn for questions and lookups. Anything that changes your PC still needs "VoxStride, execute".

**While a task runs**
- **Pause and resume** with Ctrl+Alt+P or the Pause button. Nothing new starts while paused, and Stop still works.

**AI**
- **Backup provider:** if your main AI provider is down before anything was done, VoxStride asks your backup provider instead.
