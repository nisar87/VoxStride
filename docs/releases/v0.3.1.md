## VoxStride 0.3.1: detailed diagnostics

VoxStride now keeps a detailed diagnostics log, so problems can be found quickly.

- **Every step is logged:** button presses and shortcuts, each phrase VoxStride heard (with confidence), finish-phrase decisions, every change of state and the reason for it, how long the speech engine took, the routing decision (handled on your PC or sent to the AI), each AI request (time to first response, tokens used, tools requested, errors), each tool step (result, whether it was verified, how long it took), approvals, updates, sleep and lock, and settings changes shown as before → after.
- **Errors include full details** (stack traces), including crashes and background failures that were previously silent.
- **Microphone details:** which device opened, how much audio arrived, the reason for any problem, and a check of all microphones when one is silent. Near-misses of "Hey VoxStride" are logged with their confidence only, never the words, so wake detection can be tuned for your accent.
- **A health line every 10 minutes** (memory, threads, microphone state) makes leaks and stuck states visible.
- **New: Save diagnostics report** (About or History) creates one zip with the logs, a summary of your PC and settings, and run history, ready to send.
- **The Diagnostics page** now shows the PC/settings summary and the last 60 log lines.
- **Privacy:** API keys are removed automatically from every log line, even from error messages. Audio is never logged. Your words and VoxStride's replies are included so recognition problems can be fixed. You can turn that off under Privacy. The log stays on your PC.
- The log is bigger (five files of 5 MB) and is written in the background, so it never slows VoxStride down.
