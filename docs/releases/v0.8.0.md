# VoxStride 0.8.0 — your desktop companion

VoxStride now lives on your desktop as a character you can simply talk to. It answers like a person, in real time, and does what you ask.

## New
- **Real-time conversation (Gemini Live).** Just talk. VoxStride starts answering in about a second, you can interrupt it any time, and there's no "proceed" or "execute". It uses your Gemini key.
- **The character and the bar.** A friendly animated character with headphones sits on your desktop:
  - It leans in and its headset lights turn green while it hears you, looks up while it thinks, and moves its mouth when it talks.
  - What it says appears in a speech bubble.
  - The bar underneath has a mic button (mute / unmute), a box to type instead of talking, and a menu.
  - Drag it anywhere. Resize it with Ctrl + mouse wheel, or Bigger / Smaller in the menu.
- **It just does things.** "Check the weather in France and put it in an Excel sheet" searches the web, creates the Excel file in Documents and opens it. It can also open apps, work with your files and use the browser.
- **It asks only before things that can't be undone**, like deleting, sending, paying or submitting. Answer by saying "yes" or "no", or click.
- **It can look at your screen.** "What does this error mean?", "translate this page", "help me with this level". Under Settings › Permissions, choose whether it asks each time, looks whenever it needs to, or never looks. Pictures are never saved.
- **It remembers you.** "Remember that my sister is called Ayesha." Memories stay on your PC; delete them under Voice settings › Forget everything.
- **Personality.** Set your name and how VoxStride should be (for example "cheerful, talks to me in Roman Urdu") under Voice › Live conversation.
- When Teams or Zoom uses the microphone, VoxStride mutes itself and unmutes afterwards.

## Shortcuts
- **Ctrl+Alt+V**: start talking, or mute/unmute.
- **Ctrl+Alt+X**: stop talking now.
- **Ctrl+Alt+M**: mute.
- Double-click the character to end or start the conversation.
