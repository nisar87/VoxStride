# VoxStride 0.7.5

- **"Proceed" on its own now finishes the instruction for new installs.** If you say only "proceed", "execute", "go ahead" or "do it" as a sentence by itself, VoxStride runs the instruction straight away. The words inside a sentence ("proceed with the cleanup") are still just part of what you're saying. Existing installs keep their setting: turn it on under Voice › "Saying just Proceed … finishes the instruction".
