# VoxStride 0.9.0 — characters, Twitch, screen comments and offline AI

## New
- **Six characters, each with its own look, voice and personality.**
  - Nova: warm and playful.
  - Ayaan: calm and clever, with glasses.
  - Zara: cheerful and chatty.
  - Kai: an energetic gamer buddy.
  - Sage: gentle and thoughtful.
  - Rehan: a focused professional.

  Pick one by right-clicking the character › Character, or under Settings › Voice. You can also **use your own picture**: it bobs, glows with the listening and talking colours, and moves when it speaks.
- **All 30 Gemini voices**, each with a short description of how it sounds.
- **Twitch co-host.** Enter your channel name and VoxStride reads your stream's chat (anonymously; it never posts).
  - It thanks new subs, raids and cheers, and reacts to viewers out loud by name.
  - Viewers can never make it do anything on your PC.
- **Screen comments (optional).** VoxStride glances at your screen now and then and says something when it notices something interesting. It needs "Looking at my screen: whenever it needs to".
- **Smarter speakers mode.** Without a headset, you can now interrupt VoxStride by speaking clearly louder than its voice.
- **Memory list.** See everything VoxStride remembers about you and delete items one by one.
- **Usage.** Settings shows roughly how many Gemini tokens live conversation used today.
- **Offline AI (Ollama).** Run a model on your own PC with Ollama (needs a strong graphics card). Without a Gemini key, the character works in the classic voice mode: "Hey VoxStride", dictation, then "VoxStride, execute".

## Not included
- **Voice cloning:** Gemini's voices can't be cloned, and cloning services are paid.
- **3D/anime model files:** your own picture works instead.
