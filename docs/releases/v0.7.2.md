# VoxStride 0.7.2

A human-sounding voice with just a Gemini key, and more natural conversation.

## New
- **Natural voice with Gemini.** If you have a Gemini key, VoxStride now speaks with Google's natural Gemini voices instead of the robotic Windows voice. The default is "Sulafat"; you can pick another under Voice › How VoxStride sounds. OpenAI voices still work if you have an OpenAI key. If the natural voice is slow or unavailable, VoxStride falls back to the Windows voice, so it never goes silent.
- **Talks like a person.** VoxStride now answers everything you say, not just the task. "Hi, how are you?" gets a friendly answer before it checks your drive. When you think out loud or ask "what do you think about…", it talks it through with you: its view, the reason, maybe an alternative, and a question back.
- Saving a Gemini or OpenAI key now switches on the natural voice straight away.
